Who We Are
Built on Experience.

Deer Park Auto Service opened in 1968 and has operated continuously from the same location ever since. Owner Roger Bein — a veteran with decades of hands-on mechanical experience and a background working in the oil industry — built this shop on a straightforward principle: fix the car correctly, charge a fair price, and treat every customer with respect.

That standard hasn't changed. Our staff includes fellow veterans who bring the same discipline and attention to detail to every job that comes through the door. We don't work fast because we're cutting corners — we work fast because we know what we're doing.

Industry Background

Before opening Deer Park Auto, Roger Bein spent years working in the oil industry, developing a deep understanding of mechanical systems and how vehicles are built to perform. That real-world experience shows in how he diagnoses and approaches every repair.

Veteran Staff

Several members of our team served in the military before working at Deer Park Auto. They bring the same discipline, precision, and accountability to vehicle repair that they carried in uniform.
